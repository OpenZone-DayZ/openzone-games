Chornobyl, created and fully owned by GSC Game World, and is used on the terms GSC states for
its assets in mods of other games (see `NOTICE` at the root of the repository). The licence of

`tools/make_table.py` sews it, old and faded, onto the pocket of the cloth that covers the
stalkers' table (`OpenZone_Games/gui/textures/ozg_table_cloth_co.paa`).

# The pictures of the stalker deck

The twenty pictures in this folder are **not ours**. They are cut out of the files of the
S.T.A.L.K.E.R. games, created and fully owned by GSC Game World, and are used on the terms
GSC states for its assets in mods of other games (see `NOTICE` at the root of the
repository). The licence of this mod does not cover them.

`tools/make_cards.py` reads them when it draws the `stalker` deck: the stalkers on the aces
and the kings, the mutants on the queens, the jacks and the tens.

# The pictures of the stalker deck

The twenty-two pictures in this folder are **not ours**. They show the S.T.A.L.K.E.R.
games, created and fully owned by GSC Game World, and are used on the terms GSC states for
its assets in mods of other games (see `NOTICE` at the root of the repository). The licence
of this mod does not cover them. Twenty-one are cut out of the games' own files; Faust's
is not (see its line).

`tools/make_cards.py` reads them when it draws the `stalker` deck: the stalkers on the
aces, the kings and the two jokers, the mutants on the queens, the jacks and the tens.
